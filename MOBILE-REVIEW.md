# Alpha 0.6 mobile review

Portrait gameplay keeps three lane touch regions inside the arena (↑ / ◇ / ↓). Right-hand mode puts the boss on the left; the menu can mirror the layout for left-hand use. Successful dodges trigger counters automatically. Menu pauses combat and holds Test Mode selectors, Auto, sound, Collection and the scrollable changelog.

The vertical route starts at T1 at the bottom and ends at T5 above. T2/T3 offer three choices; T4 offers two. Node details open in a popup before confirmation. Current-tier scrolling and checkpoint resume retain the normal dungeon rules.

Momentum, Bloodlust and Flame Counter emit skill notifications at hit time. Focus reports a Perfect rescued by the wider window. Guardian reports blocking a hit. Normal counters break Flow before bonus calculation.

See [ALPHA6-REVIEW.md](ALPHA6-REVIEW.md) for validation, preview links and remaining asset/device limitations. Actual observed mobile widths were 325–390 CSS pixels; requested browser viewport overrides were inconsistent. Physical phone safe-area, thumb coverage and performance still require a device check.
